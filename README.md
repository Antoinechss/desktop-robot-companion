# Desktop Robot Companion 🤖

My very first hardware project! 

In an era where most code is generated by AI, I wanted to build something from scratch. Below I document how I built a dn programmed a desktop robot companion. 

### Robot abilities (Version 1.0):

* Facial expressions
    * Library of emotions (happy, angry, sad, surprised) displayed according to the situation 
    * Random sideways gazing and blinking for a natural feel
    * Auto sleep after a period of inactibity & wake up after a sound / movement trigger
* Camera tracking & direction system (Pan/Tilt)
    * Detect people and track faces continuously
* Sound system
    * Detect lound incoming sound directions L/R and locate its source
    * Listen and reply to the human user (With an integrated LLM)
    * Set an alert sound when unfamiliar face is detected

### The hardware 🛒

| Component | Qty | Purpose |
|-----------|:---:|---------|
| Raspberry Pi 3 Model A+ | 1 | Main board: streams camera and audio to the laptop|
| Raspberry Pi Pico H | 1 | Drives the servos and the face screen |
| Raspberry Pi AI Camera | 1 | Sees faces, with detection running on the camera's chip |
| Camera flex cable, 300 mm | 1 | Expands original (200) from the base up to the head |
| microSD card, 32 GB | 1 | Holds the Pi's operating system |
| Adafruit 1.54" 240×240 IPS TFT (ST7789) | 1 | Face screen |
| Micro servo MG90D (metal gear) | 1 | Turns the head left and right (pan) |
| Micro servo SG90 | 1 | Tilts the head up and down (tilt) |
| INMP441 I2S MEMS microphone | 2 | Hear speech and locate where sounds come from (L/R) |
| Adafruit STEMMA Speaker | 1 | Plays the robot's voice |
| Pan-tilt bracket | 1 | Holds the two servos together as a neck |
| 2×18650 battery module | 1 | Powers the robot |
| 18650 Li-ion cells | 2 | Energy |
| 5 V 4 A power supply | 1 | Powers the servos during bench testing |
| Female DC barrel jack | 1 | Connects the power supply to the circuit |
| Capacitor 1000 µF, ≥ 10 V | 1 | Smooths current spikes from the servos |
| USB-A to micro-USB cable | 2 | Powers the Pi; links the Pi to the Pico |
| Perfboard, 2.54 mm pitch | 1 | Base for the permanent soldered circuit |
