# desktop-robot-companion

my first hardware project ! 

## The hardware list 🛒

|   | Component | Qty | Purpose |
|---|-----------|:---:|---------|
| 1 | Raspberry Pi 3 Model A+ | 1 | Main board: streams camera and audio to the laptop |
| 2 | Raspberry Pi Pico H | 1 | Drives the servos and the face screen |
| 3 | Raspberry Pi AI Camera | 1 | Sees faces, with detection running on the camera's chip |
| 4 | Camera flex cable, 300 mm | 1 | Expands original (200) from the base up to the head |
| 5 | microSD card, 32 GB | 1 | Holds the Pi's operating system |
| 6 | Adafruit 1.54" 240×240 IPS TFT (ST7789) | 1 | Face screen |
| 7 | Micro servo MG90D (metal gear) | 1 | Turns the head left and right (pan) |
| 8 | Micro servo SG90 | 1 | Tilts the head up and down (tilt) |
| 9 | INMP441 I2S MEMS microphone | 2 | Hear speech and locate where sounds come from (L/R) |
| 10 | Adafruit STEMMA Speaker | 1 | Plays the robot's voice |
| 11 | Pan-tilt bracket | 1 | Holds the two servos together as a neck |
| 12 | 2×18650 battery module | 1 | Powers the robot |
| 13 | 18650 Li-ion cells | 2 | Energy |
| 14 | 5 V 4 A power supply | 1 | Powers the servos during bench testing |
| 15 | Female DC barrel jack | 1 | Connects the power supply to the circuit |
| 16 | Capacitor 1000 µF, ≥ 10 V | 1 | Smooths current spikes from the servos |
| 17 | USB-A to micro-USB cable | 2 | Powers the Pi; links the Pi to the Pico |
| 18 | Perfboard, 2.54 mm pitch | 1 | Base for the permanent soldered circuit |
